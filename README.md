# OB-GYN-Doctors-services-and-diagnosis-dashboard
OB-GYN Doctors Services and Diagnosis Dashboard provides a summary of doctors’ transactions and revenue, patient volume, average consultation and waiting times, and the most frequently performed services and diagnoses. The dashboard helps identify service demand and provides insights into the healthcare needs of the community.

**BUSINESS OBJECTIVES**
-Monitor the clinic's overall revenue and transactions.
-Identify the average waiting time and consultation time per doctor.
-Identify the average waiting time and consultation time per day.
-Understand patient volume per day.
-Identify which services are performed most frequently.
-Identify the most common patient diagnoses.
-Identify which municipality have most patient.

**TOOLS AND TECHNOLOGIES**
POWER BI – Dashboard development, data visualization, and DAX measures for metric calculations.
EXCEL – Data cleaning using formulas and Power Query, calculations using Power Pivot, data extraction, and business analysis.

**KEY KPIs**
Total Revenue
Average Revenue per Day
Total Patients
Average Consultation Time
Average Consultation Time per Day
Average Waiting Time
Average Waiting Time per Day


**INSIGHT**
1. The dataset does not identify whether an appointment is an initial consultation or a follow-up visit. As a result, the analysis cannot determine which doctors manage the highest number of follow-up appointments or evaluate patient continuity of care
2. Prenatal Checkup generated both the highest revenue and the highest number of completed appointments, suggesting that it is the clinic's core service and the primary contributor to patient volume and revenue. In contrast, Postpartum Checkup recorded the fewest completed appointments and unique patients, suggesting lower demand during the reporting period.
3.A higher number of completed appointments does not necessarily result in higher revenue. Revenue generation is influenced by multiple factors, including consultation fees, service mix, and the number of completed appointments.
4. The clinic averaged approximately five unique patients per clinic day. Without information on clinic capacity, appointment schedules, or operating hours, it cannot be determined whether this represents underutilization or full utilization of available resources.
5. Consultation and waiting times are highly consistent across doctors

**RECOMMENDATION**

1.Enhance the appointment database by adding fields that identify whether an appointment is an initial consultation or a follow-up visit. This would enable future analyses of patient retention, continuity of care, and follow-up compliance.

2. Continue prioritizing promoting Prenatal Checkup by maintaining a high quality service and promoting its importance through patient education, as it is the clinic's primary revenue-generating service. At the same time, increase awareness of Postpartum Checkup by educating patients on the importance of postnatal care through health campaigns, educational materials, or social media.

3. Evaluate doctor performance using both operational metrics (completed appointments and patient volume) and financial metrics (revenue) to gain a more comprehensive understanding of clinic performance.

4. Increase digital marketing efforts by promoting each doctor's services, specialization, and expertise through the clinic's website and social media platforms. This may help improve public awareness and attract new patients.

5. Continue monitoring consultation and waiting time to maintain consistent patient experience while identifying opportunities to further improve scheduling efficiency.
